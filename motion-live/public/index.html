How to read this
  • Big number: the creator's public subscriber count. YouTube rounds it, so large channels move in big steps (for example +100K at once).
  • Sound: you hear a tune every time a count goes up, and a fanfare when a creator passes a round milestone.
  • Gaining per hour: average growth over the history we've stored (up to 24h). Views are exact, so they're the most reliable signal.
  • Heating up / Cooling down: compares the last hour of views with the daily average. It needs about 3 hours of data.
  • Next goal: the next round number and a rough time to reach it at the current pace.
Fan-made project, not affiliated with Motion or YouTube. Data comes from the YouTube Data API.
